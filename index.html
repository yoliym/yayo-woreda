About Yayo

Yayo Woreda is one of Ethiopia's most remarkable destinations, celebrated for its lush forests, rich biodiversity, coffee heritage, and breathtaking natural scenery. Located in the Oromia Region, Yayo is internationally recognized for its UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, where nature, wildlife, and local communities thrive together.

Whether you are looking for adventure, eco-tourism, bird watching, or simply a peaceful escape, Yayo offers an unforgettable experience.

Why Visit Yayo?

Ancient Forest

Explore one of Africa's most beautiful natural forests filled with rare trees and wildlife.

Coffee Heritage

Discover the birthplace of wild Arabica coffee and enjoy authentic Ethiopian coffee culture.

Wildlife

Home to colorful birds, monkeys, and hundreds of unique plant species.

UNESCO Reserve

A globally recognized biosphere reserve protecting nature for future generations.
